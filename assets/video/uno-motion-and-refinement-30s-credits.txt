UNO: Unified Self-Supervised Monocular Odometry for Platform-Agnostic Deployment
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07013
Journal: https://doi.org/10.1049/cit2.70095
30-second method illustration, 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, silent.

Fixed overview shows actual camera translation and turning in an authored courtyard.
Sequential RGB frames and camera frusta expose motion; the inset shows its view.
The eight-expert bank and Gumbel-Softmax connections explain the paper's selection
mechanism. These selections are illustrative, not outputs of the trained networks.
The backend visualization uses a small joint geometric fit with camera poses and
affine depth parameters. The first pose and one scale fix the arbitrary gauge.
Reprojected image overlays are computed from the fitted poses and scene depth.
The alignment curve is computed from that illustrative fit, not paper results.
Image/geometry consistency and joint depth/pose refinement follow Sections 3-5.

Production: Blender Cycles scene plates; Remotion composition.
Wide plates: 1440 x 810 / 12 fps, blended at 24 fps; POV: 640 x 360 / 6 fps.
