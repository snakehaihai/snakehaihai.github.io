The complete 14-tool workflow preserves recorded workpiece, screw and driver poses
from distributed_assembly_camera_aligned_20261029. The original run used dual RM75
arms; this Blender version retargets the motion to dual PiPER arms. PiPER contact
forces are not resimulated. The robot base backs off 9 cm around the third screw
and tool return; empty-hand approach/retract motions are adjusted for clearance.
Parallel-jaw symmetry avoids wrist limits while retaining held TCP positions.

Main view: 15 fps. Head RGB-D and both rigid wrist cameras: 5 Hz samples.
Boxes use visible instance masks, not learned object detection. Recorded Mission
and Object Graph data are retained. Animation geometry is checked separately from
the source dynamics, with active grip surfaces and mounting interfaces declared.
