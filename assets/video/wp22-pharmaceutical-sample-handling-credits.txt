NTU-A*STAR joint project
WP 2.2 Spatially-Grounded Perception and Planning for Embodied AI Agents

Application 2: pharmaceutical sample handling.
Robot: AgileX Ranger Mini 3.0, lifting carriage and two RealMan RM75 arms.
Recorded run: pharma_graph_lab_20261029_v2.

The full 15-step pellet-retaining training work order covers collection,
barcode verification, uncapping, wash dosing, mixing, balanced centrifuge
loading, target preparation during centrifugation, gated unloading,
supernatant removal, resuspension, aliquoting, inspection, finished-slot
registration, material return and cleanup.

Graph as Policy uses a deterministic mission dependency graph with observed
object bindings and equipment gates. Target preparation and centrifuge
execution are parallel branches. Unloading requires both completed
preparation and stopped/unlocked equipment. The Interaction Graph derives
relations from recorded commands, wait phases, rigid object attachments and
symbolic device states. Object memory grows from rendered semantic RGB-D
observations with a known catalog and registered manipulation fixtures.

Presentation: full main-camera video with Mission Graph, Interaction Graph,
robot RGB-D, incremental Object Graph and matching-camera 3D Object Graph.
Duration: 337.2 seconds. Resolution: 1600 x 1000. Frame rate: 15 fps.
No audio. Entire timeline retained in the web encode.

Scope: kinematic training simulation with prescribed robot motion and symbolic
liquid, barcode, balance, inspection and device acknowledgements. No fluid,
contact-force or production-process validation is implied. Demonstration
program times are not production parameters. Cross-embodiment evaluation
remains a project objective.
