MMAUD: 30-second dataset demonstration

Shenghai Yuan et al., ICRA 2024.
MMAUD: A Comprehensive Multi-Modal Anti-UAV Dataset for Modern Miniature Drone Threats.
Original project: https://github.com/ntu-aris/MMAUD
Format: 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, 720 frames, 30.00 seconds.

The opening includes the original outdoor collection photograph and complete
sensing-rig photograph from the SIT Applied Research Seminar, plus an original
MMAUD stereo camera observation. The rig photograph is displayed upright.

Camera excerpts and Leica reference profiles cover Mavic 2, Mavic 3, Phantom 4,
Avata and M300 recordings. Audio waveform and spectrum come from the original
mixed field recording. LiDAR panels use original M300 background returns;
mmWave panels retain raw sparse single-scan detections.

Separate 3D flight regimes, visual stress cases, urban sound-source geometry,
target sequences and added red LiDAR interference illustrate the observation
conditions discussed in SIT seminar slides 15-17. They do not report detector
performance or isolate individual acoustic sources from the recorded mixture.

Rendered in Blender and composed in Remotion, October 2026.
