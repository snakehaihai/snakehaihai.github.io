Authored Blender 3D method illustration. Trajectories, signal traces, window changes and power profiles are explanatory animations, not experimental measurements or algorithm benchmarks.
Two guardians estimate and encircle an aerial target. When it continues approaching the protected vehicle, a guardian makes contact; the target loses attitude and falls to the ground. The contact, recoil and descent are authored animation keyframes.
Original manuscript PDFs, source models and private experiment files are not included in this publication.
