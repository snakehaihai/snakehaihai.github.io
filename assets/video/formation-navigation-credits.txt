MDS-FGS / Distributed Formation Navigation — 3D method illustration

Paper: Distributed Formation Navigation for Quadruped and Wheeled Robots via Artificial Potential Fields
Bo Zhang, Weili Chen, Chunyang Xu, Ruocheng Li, Chenxi Deng, Shiyu Chen, Shenghai Yuan, Bin Xin
IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2026, Early Access
https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2026.3746317

Original scene, wheeled robot geometry, motion illustration and composition created with Blender and Remotion.
Method explanation follows the official abstract and the author's supplied MDS-FGS clarification.
This four-robot scene is a method illustration, separate from the paper's three-platform physical experiments.
The figures 94.5% and 31.7% are reported paper results; they are not measurements from this animation.
