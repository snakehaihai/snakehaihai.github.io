AIR-Embodied: 15-second 3D method demonstration

The complete approved 30-second Remotion animation is played at 2x speed.
The animation illustrates viewpoint selection, object interaction and rescanning.
The planning panel and reconstruction are a scripted method visualization.

Method: https://doi.org/10.1142/S2301385027410019
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16019
UR5e geometry and kinematics: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/universal_robots_ur5e
The laboratory, target object, motion, captions and sound design were created for this demonstration.
Robot shading, materials and tool attachment were adapted for the rendered scene.
