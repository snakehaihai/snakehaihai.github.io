GP-UWB / Large-Scale UWB Anchor Calibration and One-Shot Localization Using Gaussian Process
Shenghai Yuan, Boyang Lou, Thien-Minh Nguyen, Pengyu Yin, Muqing Cao, Xinghang Xu, Jianping Li, Jie Xu, Siyu Chen, Lihua Xie
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16880
https://github.com/luobodan/GP-UWB-calibration

Original 30-second Blender / Remotion method illustration, 1920 x 1080, 24 fps.
The wide campus scene remains visible throughout, with a separate moving-operator acquisition close-up.
An original campus-inspired environment and ATV sensor rig are used, not a measured reconstruction of the experiment site.
Synthetic poses and UWB ranges are incrementally fitted with a Matern Gaussian Process.
The response transform follows the public calibration implementation: 1 / log10(sqrt(range) + 1).
Learned response maxima provide the illustrated anchor-position estimates; the uncertainty plot is relative GP predictive standard deviation.
This progressive data-prefix illustration is not a claim of online calibration or a paper benchmark.
The close-up presents a separate collection segment at normal speed; the overview condenses acquisition time.
No original manuscript, slide deck, private notes, or raw experiment data are included.
