The actual NTU survey point-cloud map is reproduced from the author's supplied
CVlife_Lou_UWB_achor_mapping slides (embedded image198.png).
Viridis colors follow the Gaussian Process heatmap figures in the same slides.
The trajectory is traced along visible campus roads for this explanation.
Synthetic UWB observations and prefix-only Matern GP updates illustrate how
additional trajectory coverage refines the range-response field and anchor estimates.
The background survey map does not enter GP fitting. The animation is not a
replay of experimental predictions or a quantitative benchmark.
Response transform: 1 / log10(sqrt(range) + 1).
The close-up retains the original Blender ATV/operator animation; this is a
separate illustrative acquisition segment. Overview acquisition time is condensed.
