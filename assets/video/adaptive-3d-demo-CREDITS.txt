Adaptive 3D Perception: 30-second method demonstration

The complete approved animation is presented at its original speed.
Format: 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, 720 frames, 30.00 seconds.

Method: Adaptive 3D Perception for Small Aerial Targets Under Sparse Sampling
via Reinforcement Learning, Shenghai Yuan et al., CVPR 2026.

Environment and target points are drawn from MMAUD V2/V3 recordings.
The sensing rig follows the MMAUD layout: upward Avia, level MID-360,
and stereo cameras. The scene uses measured structural coordinates with
photo-guided completion of the facade, parking area and vegetation.

The aircraft appearance, red transient noise, temporal charts and policy
actions illustrate the method. The animation does not execute a trained PPO
policy or report detector accuracy. Recorded target range is preserved;
source flight time is compressed within the approved 30-second animation.

Rendered in Blender and composed in Remotion, October 2026.
