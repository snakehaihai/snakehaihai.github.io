Energy-Constrained Navigation for Planetary Rovers under Hybrid RTG-Solar Power
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15062
30-second 3D method illustration, 1920 x 1080, 24 fps.
Blender Cycles scene animation with Remotion explanation and comparison overlays.

Two illustrative runs use identical rover hardware, start/goal, initial mission
energy allowance, fixed RTG-solar supply and instantaneous power limit.
The direct route climbs the ridge, stalls at an infeasible climb and expends its
remaining allowance during repeated attempts. The lower route avoids unnecessary
ascent and descent, reaches the shared goal and retains energy.
The terrain, routes, stalled motion and normalized resource traces are authored
for explaining the method. They are not experimental results or measured paper
benchmarks. The simple explanatory model includes a baseline subsystem load,
rolling resistance and the additional cost of uphill motion; downhill potential
energy is not recovered. Time is compressed.

Visual assets:
- Newly authored lunar ridge/corridor terrain, non-repeating procedural regolith,
  irregular basalt fragments and wheel tracks.
- Existing original articulated six-wheel rover and RTG hardware from the local
  approved research-film scene, with terrain-aligned chassis and wheel suspension.
