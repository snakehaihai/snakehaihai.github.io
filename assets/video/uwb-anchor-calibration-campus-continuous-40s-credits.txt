GP-UWB: Large-Scale UWB Anchor Calibration and One-Shot Localization Using Gaussian Process
S. Yuan, B. Lou et al. | ICRA 2025
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16880

40-second continuous NTU campus multi-view. Blender Cycles + Remotion, 1920x1080, 24 fps.
Wide rotating campus reconstruction with a semitransparent cyan GP response surface.
Upper left: recorded ntu_day_01 D455b colour camera, matched by timestamp to the displayed reference trajectory.
Upper right: original third-person ATV reconstruction based on the author's supplied vehicle photograph.
One continuous interval from T+58.0 to T+589.122 seconds, uniformly time-compressed by approximately 13.3x. No excerpt cuts or route jumps.
All GP updates retain previously accumulated observations; no future samples are used.
Recorded camera timestamps are matched within 0.05 seconds of the requested source time.
The follow-camera heading is eased; obstructing building geometry fades smoothly in the inset to preserve vehicle visibility. Camera colours are retained from the recording. Third-person camera and vehicle geometry are authored illustration.

Buildings and roads: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Visual reference: https://ntumap.app/ and the author's supplied campus screenshots.
Building heights, facades, terrain and tropical vegetation are authored reconstruction.
The close-up uses wider authored carriageways along the existing OSM centre-lines, without trajectory fitting.
Map registration: existing map-only rigid SE(2) candidate, not independently surveyed positioning accuracy.
Route, true anchors, fitted centres and heatmap share the same world frame and camera projection.

Public trajectory and UWB ranges: GP-UWB-calibration / MCDUWB / ntu_day_01.
The GP explanatory refit uses reference poses and ranges, not the paper's original LiDAR-inertial benchmark predictions.
Each planar Matern GP models inverse-log-square-root range response using a chronological sample prefix.
Estimated centres are maxima of the fitted response. Anchor ground truth does not enter fitting or peak search.
The cyan terrain overlay shows the maximum fitted response across anchors, with confidence-weighted opacity.
White squares: true anchors. Cyan crosshairs: estimated centres. Gold: collected reference route.
Ground marker XY positions are preserved; terrain heights are illustrative.
