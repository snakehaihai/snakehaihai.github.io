GP-UWB: Large-Scale UWB Anchor Calibration and One-Shot Localization Using Gaussian Process
S. Yuan, B. Lou et al. | ICRA 2025
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16880
https://github.com/luobodan/GP-UWB-calibration

30-second NTU campus reconstruction. Blender Cycles + Remotion, 1920x1080, 24 fps.
Buildings and roads: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Visual reference: https://ntumap.app/ and the author's supplied campus screenshots.
No NTU Map meshes, textures, or proprietary application assets were copied.
Building heights, facade details, terrain and tropical vegetation are authored for illustration.
Map registration: existing map-only rigid SE(2) candidate from the author's
mapping workspace. Fixed unit scale; no fitting to trajectory predictions or anchor estimates.
Map, reference trajectory, true anchor positions and inferred centres use the same
NTU world frame and the same Blender camera projection.

Public trajectory and UWB ranges: GP-UWB-calibration / MCDUWB / ntu_day_01.
The reference trajectory is used as calibration input for this explanatory GP refit.
This is not a replay of the paper's LiDAR-inertial predictions or its original benchmark.
The planar Matérn GP refit uses inverse-log-square-root range response and chronological prefixes.
Anchor ground truth is displayed separately and does not enter GP fitting or peak search.
Ground markers preserve anchor XY coordinates; reconstructed terrain elevations are illustrative.
Map registration residuals are not independent surveyed positioning accuracy.
Acquisition time is condensed; main camera remains at campus-wide scale.

Acquisition inset: original Blender ATV/operator illustration.
