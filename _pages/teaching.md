## Teaching

**Introduction to Aerial Robotics — NTU EEE outreach, 2022–present.** Lectures and hands-on sessions introduce prospective students to aerial robots, sensing, autonomy, and robotic system design.

**Engineering Mathematics (EE2006) — Teaching Assistant, 2015–2016.** Tutorials covered linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, Fourier analysis, Laplace transforms, and numerical methods. I also developed an online scoring and marking system to support assessment and feedback.

## Project supervision and research mentorship

- **PhD research, 2019–present:** co-mentored more than 12 PhD students and research projects in planning, control, perception, SLAM, and robot learning.
- **MSc dissertations, 2019–present:** co-supervised more than 25 projects, from problem definition and implementation to experiments and publication.
- **Undergraduate final-year projects (EE4080), 2015–present:** guided 29 NTU EEE/REP project groups and delivered practical robotics workshops.
- **Design and Innovation Projects (EE2073 / EE3180), 2015–present:** developed robotics, automation, and IoT project topics and mentored student teams.

## Technical workshops

I have organized eight workshops and tutorials on ROS, robotics software development, experimental workflow, research collaboration, and academic writing. Student-led projects developed through this mentorship have produced more than 60 accepted papers in venues including ICRA, IROS, CVPR, AAAI, and IEEE RA-L.
