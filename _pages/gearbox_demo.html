These are fixed-mount stage tests initialized from recorded states, with illustrative motor and contact parameters. Parts move through integrated contact forces, with no grasp attachment or runtime object-pose resets. Of 16 nominal tests, 0/7 grasps, 2/6 insertions and 0/3 fastening stages pass; six additional timestep/control checks complete the 22-case audit. The complete PiPER assembly sequence has not yet passed contact validation.

Screw advance uses a passive captive helical joint. Thread-flank stress and clamp load are outside this model. Initial support-hand overlaps invalidate some insertion and screw cases; those recordings are retained as diagnostics.
