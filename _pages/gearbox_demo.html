Planetary gearbox assembly

Ranger Mini 3.0 · Dual PiPER arms · Simulation

A mobile dual-arm robot explores the workcell, builds an object graph and selects a task-dependent chain of objects. It collects gear components from separate tables, assembles the gearbox, retrieves a wall-mounted driver, fastens the cover and returns the tool.
