Dr. Shenghai Yuan

Nanyang Technological University.

I received my B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My research focuses on robotics perception and navigation. Currently, I am a senior research fellow at the Centre for Advanced Robotics Technology Innovation (CARTIN) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My research interest lies in robotics, with a focus on SLAM, sensor fusion, and autonomous navigation in challenging environments such as mines and dynamic human-robot spaces, aiming to advance adaptive and collaborative robotic systems.

I have authored or co-authored more than 200 peer-reviewed publications in robotics, computer vision, and AI, and contributed to more than 10 patents and other intellectual property outputs. I serve as an Associate Editor for Pattern Recognition, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE), and Unmanned Systems, and as an Area Chair for RSS 2027 and a Senior Program Committee Member for AAAI 2027.

My service has been recognized with Outstanding Reviewer Awards from ICRA (2024), CVPR (2025), IEEE RA-L (2026), ECCV (2026), and NeurIPS (2026), and Best Associate Editor Awards from Unmanned Systems (2025 and 2026). Our work has also received the IROS 2023 Best Entertainment and Amusement Paper Award and the Best Poster Award at the ICRA 2026 Robot Meets Prior Map Workshop.

    Nanyang Technological University, Singapore
    Abs Bib HTML PDF Demo

    G2-Nav translates social reasoning from a vision-language model into grounded costmaps for robot navigation. Tracked agents and scene regions shape the local route, while visual verification checks depth registration and a fast LiDAR reflex responds to newly observed obstacles between model updates.
